Operations automations for a membership association

Automation

Demo build · Five n8n workflows for intake, approvals, onboarding and reporting
  • Inquiry intake: one form sorts membership, event and general inquiries into their own sheets, sends the person an acknowledgement and alerts the right team.
  • Membership approval: the approver gets Approve and Reject buttons by email, the decision is logged, and the applicant is told automatically.
  • Onboarding and check-ins: new members get a welcome email and join a tracker, then a daily run sends 7-day and 30-day check-ins and marks each one as sent.
  • Daily dashboard refresh: pulls members, inquiries, tasks and events into five KPIs, including renewals due in the next 30 days and open tasks.
  • Weekly management report: every Monday, management gets an email summary of last week's new members, inquiries and event sign-ups.
